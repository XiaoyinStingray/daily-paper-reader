## 摘要
余弦变换因其计算简便且能量压缩能力强，在图像和视频压缩中被频繁使用。这激发了光学界对以光学方式计算余弦变换以用于压缩的兴趣。当在傅里叶平面成像以捕获余弦变换时，会遇到相位恢复问题：光场同时具有幅度和相位，但相机仅能捕获场的幅度。余弦变换将相位恢复问题限制在二值域而非单位圆上，但一般的相位恢复方法并未利用这一点。在本工作中，我们证明余弦变换相位恢复中的符号错误会导致该变换在错误处的 Hessian 幅度显著增大。受此启发，我们开发了一种通过最小化重建余弦变换的曲率来求解该二值相位恢复问题的算法。我们通过计算实验表明，给定图像余弦变换的幅度，我们能够稳定地重建原始图像，多尺度结构相似性指数达到 0.95。我们表明，求解时间随所求解像素数近似线性增长。

## Abstract
Cosine transforms see frequent use in image and video compression due to their ease of computation and high energy compaction. This has sparked interest within the optics community in computing cosine transforms optically for compression. When imaging in the Fourier plane to capture a cosine transform, one encounters a phase retrieval problem: optical fields have both magnitude and phase, but cameras only capture the magnitude of the field. Cosine transforms restrict the phase retrieval problem to a binary domain as opposed to the unit circle, but general phase retrieval solutions do not leverage this. In this work, we demonstrate that sign errors in phase retrieval for cosine transforms result in a significant increase in the magnitude of the Hessian of the transform at the error. Motivated by this, we develop an algorithm to solve this binary phase retrieval problem by minimizing the curvature of the reconstructed cosine transform. We demonstrate via computational experiments that given the magnitude of the cosine transform of an image, we can consistently reconstruct the original image within a multiscale structural similarity index of 0.95. We show that the solve time grows approximately linearly with the number of pixels solved.
