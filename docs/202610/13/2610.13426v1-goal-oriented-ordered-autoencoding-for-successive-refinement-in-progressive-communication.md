## 摘要
面向目标的通信在可变且不可预测的带宽下，往往难以维持下游任务性能。我们通过提出 GO-OAE（Goal-Oriented Ordered AutoEncoding，面向目标的有序自编码）来解决该问题；GO-OAE 学习一个可渐进细化的量化潜在序列，并直接面向任务效用而非源重建进行优化。序列的每个分量在传输时由其码索引表示，学习到的顺序使得能够从索引序列的任意已接收前缀进行推理，且随着更多索引到达，性能不断提升。我们证明，优化面向目标的失真目标等价于求解一个加权条件信息瓶颈表述，并引入一种无偏多前缀重要性采样（MPIS）估计器，将 GO-OAE 训练计算成本从 O(BK) 降至 O(Bm)，其中 m << K，并将估计器方差降低为原来的 1/m。实验表明，在 MiniImageNet 上，GO-OAE 以每幅图像 324 比特（相对于原始 RGB 的 683 倍压缩）实现了无损上界分类准确率的 96% 以上，并且仅使用 70% 的传输索引就已达到上界的 94% 以上。在所有传输预算下，它都持续优于重建优化的编解码器以及使用均匀前缀采样训练的有序自编码器，展示了在极端压缩率下的有效通信。

## Abstract
Goal-oriented communication often struggles to sustain downstream task performance under variable and unpredictable bandwidth. We address this problem by proposing GO-OAE (Goal-Oriented Ordered AutoEncoding), which learns a progressively refinable quantized latent sequence, optimized directly for task utility rather than source reconstruction. Each component of the sequence is represented for transmission by its code index, and the learned ordering enables inference from any received prefix of the index sequence, with performance improving as more indices arrive. We prove that optimizing the goal-oriented distortion objective is equivalent to solving a weighted conditional information bottleneck formulation, and introduce an unbiased multi-prefix importance sampling (MPIS) estimator, to reduce GO-OAE training computational cost from O(BK) to O(Bm) with m << K, and reduces the estimator variance by a factor of 1/m. Experiments show that, on MiniImageNet, GO-OAE achieves over 96% of the lossless upper-bound classification accuracy using 324 bits per image (683X compression vs. raw RGB), and already attains over 94% of the upper bound with only 70% of the transmitted indices. It consistently outperforms reconstruction-optimized codecs and ordered autoencoders trained with uniform prefix sampling across all transmission budgets, demonstrating effective communication at extreme compression rates.
