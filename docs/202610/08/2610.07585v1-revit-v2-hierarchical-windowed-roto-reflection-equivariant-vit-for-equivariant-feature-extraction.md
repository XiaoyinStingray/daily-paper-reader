## 摘要
我们提出了一种可扩展的旋转-反射群等变视觉Transformer，基于窗口化群卷积自注意力和分层特征架构。我们证明，我们的方法可以扩展到具有数百万参数的群等变视觉Transformer（ViT）以及具有实际尺寸图像的大型数据集，即ImageNet。所提出的层次化窗口旋转-反射等变ViT（REViT-v2）的代码和预训练权重可在 https://github.com/kc-ml2/revit 获取。

## Abstract
We propose a scalable roto-reflection-group-equivariant vision transformer based on windowed group-convolutional self-attention and a hierarchical feature architecture. We demonstrate that our approach can be scaled to group-equivariant vision transformers (ViTs) with millions of parameters and large datasets with practically sized images, i.e., ImageNet. The code and pretrained weights for the proposed Hierarchical Windowed Roto-reflection Equivariant ViTs (REViT-v2) are available at https://github.com/kc-ml2/revit.
