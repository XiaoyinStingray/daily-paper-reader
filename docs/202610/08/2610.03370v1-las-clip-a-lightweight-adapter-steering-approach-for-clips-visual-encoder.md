## 摘要
CLIP 的视觉编码器仅产生全局图像表示，限制了其在区域级任务中的应用。现有适配方法依赖视觉提示、输入掩码或编码器微调，但都会损害预训练表示。我们提出 LAS-CLIP，一种轻量级适配器引导方法，保持所有 CLIP 参数冻结。一个紧凑的 MaskAdapter 根据输入掩码生成逐头、逐层的注意力偏置，并将其注入冻结的自注意力层，从而将注意力引导至目标区域。关键在于，由于主干网络严格保持不变，当不提供掩码时，LAS-CLIP 可无缝回退到原始 CLIP，保留其基础零样本能力。在两张 T4 GPU 上，仅约 116K 至 145K 个可训练参数和 10 万训练样本的情况下，LAS-CLIP 在 ImageNet-S 零样本分类和 RefCOCO 指代表达理解任务上取得了与 Alpha-CLIP 相当或更优的结果，而后者需在数百万样本上微调整个编码器。定性分析进一步证实，在错误掩码下以及下游生成中，该方法具有更强的表示保真度。我们的项目页面链接为 https://github.com/AnhKhoa585/lasclip

## Abstract
CLIP's visual encoder produces only global image representations, limiting its use in region-level tasks. Existing adaptations rely on visual prompting, input masking, or encoder fine-tuning, each compromising pre-trained representations. We propose LAS-CLIP, a Lightweight Adapter Steering approach that keeps every CLIP parameter frozen. A compact MaskAdapter generates per-head, per-layer attention biases from an input mask and injects them into the frozen self-attention layers, steering attention toward the target region. Crucially, because the backbone remains strictly untouched, LAS-CLIP seamlessly reverts to vanilla CLIP when no mask is provided, preserving its foundational zero-shot capabilities. With approximately 116K to 145K trainable parameters and 100K training samples on two T4 GPUs, LAS-CLIP achieves competitive or superior results compared to Alpha-CLIP on ImageNet-S zero-shot classification and RefCOCO referring expression comprehension, despite the latter fine-tuning its entire encoder on millions of samples.
